# Free video production options (reviewed 2026-10-13)

Free-plan limits can change by account, region, and time. Check each service's own plan page before relying on a feature.

## Recommended first try: CapCut
- Official AI video generator: https://www.capcut.com/tools/free-ai-video-generator
- The official page advertises text-to-video and AI script/voiceover features on a free tier.
- Availability and export options can vary by region/account; test one short draft before planning a full long-form video.

## Good final assembly option: Microsoft Clipchamp
- Official editor: https://app.clipchamp.com/
- Official support: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/clipchamp/how-to-use-the-text-to-speech-feature
- The personal free plan supports editing with own/free stock media and exports up to 1080p without a watermark. Text-to-speech languages and features depend on the available voice list/account.
- Useful for combining clips, narration, subtitles, music and title cards. It is an editor, not an unlimited text-to-video engine.

## Limited free credits: InVideo AI
- Official free-plan details: https://help.invideo.io/en/articles/9380226-can-i-use-invideo-ai-for-free
- The free plan provides limited weekly credits; AI generation is credit-limited. Check export watermark and feature restrictions in your account before committing.

## YouTube tutorials
- Search results can contain exaggerated claims such as "unlimited free, no watermark." Do not assume a video title proves the offer is real. Always confirm terms on the tool's official site.
- Example tutorial result (use as inspiration, not as proof of pricing): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IHWaCFfvK_g

## Suggested workflow for this project
1. Use the local Ollama script generator to create a first Urdu script draft.
2. Research and verify factual claims manually; the local model does not browse the web.
3. Generate/collect visuals with tools whose licenses allow YouTube use.
4. Use Clipchamp or CapCut to create narration, subtitles, and edit the video.
5. Or put licensed still images in the project's visuals folder and narration audio in narration.mp3, then use scripts/build-video.ps1 to make a 1080p slideshow MP4.
6. Watch the full export and finish final-review.md before you upload manually.

## YouTube AI disclosure
YouTube requires disclosure for realistic synthetic/altered content that could mislead viewers, such as a real person appearing to say something they did not say or a realistic scene that did not happen. Ordinary production assistance such as AI script drafts does not itself require disclosure under the cited guidance. Check the current YouTube Help page when uploading:
https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491
